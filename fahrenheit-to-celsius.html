    Questions

    What is the formula?

    Celsius = (Fahrenheit − 32) × 5 ÷ 9. Fahrenheit = Celsius × 9 ÷ 5 + 32.

    Is 100 °F a fever?

    100 °F is 37.8 °C. A temperature of 100.4 °F (38 °C) or more is usually counted as a fever.

    What is 350 °F in an oven?

    350 °F is about 177 °C, usually set as 180 °C.
