Questions

What is the VAT rate in Egypt?

14% since July 2017 (it started at 13% in 2016). Some goods and services have other rates or are exempt. It is run by the Egyptian Tax Authority.

How do I take 14% VAT out of a price?

Divide by 1.14. A total of EGP 1,140 is EGP 1,000 before VAT, so the VAT is EGP 140.

How do I add 14% VAT?

Multiply by 1.14. EGP 1,000 plus VAT is EGP 1,140.

When must a business register for VAT?

When its sales pass EGP 500,000 a year.
