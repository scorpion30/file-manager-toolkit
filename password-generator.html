Password Generator

Strong random passwords, made in your browser. Nothing is sent or saved.

More passwords

    How to use Password Generator

    1. Pick the length: 16 or more is a good choice.
    2. Choose the characters: upper and lower case, numbers and symbols.
    3. Copy the password, or click one from the list below. Press ↻ for a new one.

    Questions

    Is it safe to make a password here?

    Yes. The password is made in your browser with crypto.getRandomValues, the secure random generator built into it. It is never sent anywhere or saved.

    How long should a password be?

    At least 12 characters, and 16 or more for email and banking. Length matters more than symbols.

    How do I remember it?

    Use a password manager, like the one built into your browser or phone. Use a different password for every site.
