      How to use Rotate PDF

      1. Select your PDFs.
      2. Choose the angle and which pages: all of them, only landscape or portrait pages, or a list.
      3. Rotate and download. The pages are saved turned, so they open the right way everywhere.

      Questions

      Is the rotation saved?

      Yes. The new orientation is saved in the PDF, so it opens the right way in every reader and prints that way.

      Can I rotate only the landscape pages?

      Yes. Choose "Landscape" to turn only pages that are wider than tall, for example drawings in a report.
