    How to use Liters to Gallons

    1. Type a number in either box. The other box updates as you type.
    2. Pick other units from the lists, or press ⇄ to swap them.
    3. Copy any result: click a value in the list to copy it.

    Questions

    Is a US gallon the same as a UK gallon?

    No. A US gallon is 3.785 litres and a UK (imperial) gallon is 4.546 litres. Pick the one you need from the list.

    Is this converter free?

    Yes, free and without sign-up. It works in your browser, even offline once the page has loaded.
