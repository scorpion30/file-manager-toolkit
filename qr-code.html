The QR code holds your link or text itself, not a redirect, so it keeps working forever. Test it with your phone camera before you print.

How to use QR Code Generator

  1. Pick what the QR code opens: a link, Wi-Fi, WhatsApp, a phone number, an email or a contact card.
  2. Fill in the details. Change the colours or add your logo if you like.
  3. Download the PNG for screens or the SVG for printing at any size.

Questions

Do these QR codes expire?

Never. The QR code holds your link or text itself. Some sites make "dynamic" QR codes that go through their server and stop working when you stop paying. These don’t.

Can I add my logo?

Yes. Choose a logo and it is placed in the middle. Error correction is set to High so the code still scans.

How does the Wi-Fi QR code work?

Guests point their phone camera at it and join your network without typing the password.
