How to use Text Case Converter

  1. Paste or type your text in the box.
  2. Click a button: UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, or a clean-up like removing extra spaces.
  3. Copy the result or download it as a .txt file. Undo goes back one step.

Questions

What is Title Case?

Every word starts with a capital letter, like a book title: "The Quick Brown Fox". Sentence case only capitalises the first word of each sentence.

Can it remove the diacritics from Arabic text?

Yes. "Remove Arabic diacritics" takes out the tashkeel (fatha, damma, kasra, shadda and the rest) and the tatweel, and keeps the letters.

Can it count words and characters?

Yes. The Counter shows characters with and without spaces, words, sentences, lines and reading time, and updates as you type.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved.
