Rename from Title Block

Name every PDF after what's written in its title block, like the drawing number and title.

Your files stay on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.

This browser can't rename files on your computer. You'll get a ZIP with the renamed copies instead. To rename in place, use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer.
