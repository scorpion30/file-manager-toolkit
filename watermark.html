      How to use Add Watermark

      1. Select your PDFs.
      2. Type the text or choose your logo, then set the position, size, angle and transparency. The preview shows the first page.
      3. Add the watermark and download.

      Questions

      Can I write the watermark in Arabic?

      Yes. Any language works, including Arabic, with the letters joined correctly.

      Over or under the content?

      "Over" is always visible. "Under" sits behind text and drawings like a light background, but it can be hidden on scanned pages, which are pictures.
