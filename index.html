Step-by-step guides →

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. The tools run in your browser. Files are read from your computer and the results are saved back to it. You can even disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Is it really free? What are the limits?

It is free, with no account and no limits on the number of files or tasks. The only limit is your computer's memory with very large files.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. After you have used a tool once, the page and its PDF engine are kept by your browser, so the tool opens and works offline.

Which browser should I use?

Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer can also save results straight into a folder and rename files in place. Other browsers work too, and give you the result as a download.

Does OCR work in Arabic?

Yes. OCR PDF and PDF to Excel read English, Arabic or both.
