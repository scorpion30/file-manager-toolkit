      How to use Crop PDF

      1. Select your PDFs.
      2. Drag a box on the page around what you want to keep, or choose "Trim white margins".
      3. Crop and download.

      Questions

      Is the cropped part deleted?

      It is hidden: every PDF reader shows and prints only the area you kept. The original content is still inside the file.

      Can I trim different margins on each page?

      Yes. "Trim white margins" finds the content on each page separately and cuts around it.
