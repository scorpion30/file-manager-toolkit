    How to use Miles to Km

    1. Type a number in either box. The other box updates as you type.
    2. Pick other units from the lists, or press ⇄ to swap them.
    3. Copy any result: click a value in the list to copy it.

    Questions

    How many kilometres is a mile?

    One mile is 1.609344 km. To change miles to km, multiply by 1.609344.

    How do I change mph to km/h?

    The same way: multiply by 1.609344. 60 mph is 96.6 km/h.
