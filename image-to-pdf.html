      How to use JPG to PDF

      1. Select your images or a folder of them. JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP and TIFF all work.
      2. Put them in order by dragging, or sort by name or date.
      3. Choose the page size and margins, then convert and download the PDF.

      Questions

      Will my phone photos be sideways?

      No. The rotation saved by the phone camera is read, so every photo is placed the right way up.

      Does converting lower the quality?

      Not with "Original": JPG photos are placed in the PDF exactly as they are. "Smaller file" reduces them to 150 DPI for email.

      Can I make one PDF for each folder?

      Yes. Select a folder with subfolders and choose "A PDF per folder": each folder becomes its own PDF.
