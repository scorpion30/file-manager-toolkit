      How to use Organize Pages

      1. Select one or more PDFs. All their pages appear as pictures.
      2. Drag pages into the order you want, rotate or delete them, or add a blank page.
      3. Save the new PDF.

      Questions

      Can I combine pages from different PDFs?

      Yes. Add several PDFs: all their pages appear together, and you can mix them in any order.

      Can I undo a deleted page?

      Click "Start over", or add the file again. Nothing changes in your original files.
